About FlyLab

167,106 simulated neurons and 35,310 type-shared internal parameters. Joint learning updates the brain and a 2,052-parameter linear action readout together. Verified tasks and checkpoints are listed in the level menu; Results shows their measured outcomes. The four-level pilot and three B00 repair checkpoints remain available. Connection endpoints and effective signs stay fixed.

Each board drives a fresh 40 ms simulation. The policy reads 512 pooled neural features; it has no direct board input. Output correction is disabled. This tests parameter learning, not persistent neural-state memory.

Step change compares consecutive inputs. Learning change compares the selected checkpoint with the brain before its training stage. Each selected model uses its own saved readout. Traces record actual spikes and voltage; anatomical routes show a subset of the full simulated network.

Playback uses saved checkpoints. Playing a level does not update the weights.
