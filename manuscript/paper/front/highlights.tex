\section{Highlights}
\begin{itemize}
  \item A fixed whole-fly connectome learns to plan and to remember a visual cue
  \item The cue is held silently in neuronal adaptation and read out by the next input
  \item Recall works only in the brain state of learning, at writing and at reading
  \item A disease-like lesion rewrites the trace, while learning in the new state is spared
\end{itemize}
