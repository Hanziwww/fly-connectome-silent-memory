# Data sources

The project uses MaleCNS v1.0 and its companion anatomical resources. FlyBrain is the simulator, not a separate biological dataset.

| Resource | Use | Location |
|---|---|---|
| `body-annotations-male-cns-v1.0-minconf-0.5.feather` | Neuron identities, types, classes, soma coordinates and parameter grouping | `flybrain/data/` |
| `body-neurotransmitters-male-cns-v1.0.feather` | Predicted/consensus transmitter labels, mapped to effective signs by simulator rules | `flybrain/data/` |
| `connectome-weights-male-cns-v1.0-minconf-0.5.feather` | Directed connectivity and synapse-count weights | `flybrain/data/` |
| `fullbrain-roi-v4`, `malecns-vnc-neuropil-roi-v0` | Labeled brain and ventral nerve cord surface meshes | `flybrain/data/anatomy/` |
| `skeletons-malecns/skeletons-precomputed` | Individual neuron centerline morphology | `flybrain/data/morphology/` |
| `male-cns-v1.0-synapses-precomputed` | Contact coordinates joining neuron skeleton paths | `flybrain/data/morphology/` |

The three core downloads have source URLs, sizes and upstream-matching hashes in the [download receipt](data-sources/download-receipt.json). Geometry provenance and omissions are recorded in [anatomy sources](../sokoban/artifacts/anatomy/sources.json), [anatomy metadata](../sokoban/artifacts/anatomy/metadata.json), and [morphology metadata](../sokoban/artifacts/morphology/metadata.json).

The full simulator has 167,106 neurons. Anatomical rendering includes 114 regions and 140,024 available soma locations. The detailed activity/morphology sample has 4,224 cells; 9,913 of 12,000 sampled connection routes have supported skeleton/contact paths. Missing positions and unsupported routes are omitted. Region surface colors identify anatomical categories, not a per-neuron region assignment.

Spikes, membrane potentials, animated activity and learned modulation are generated by the simulator. Particle transit visualizes its lumped delay, not a measured conduction velocity. Sokoban levels and teaching trajectories are locally constructed; there is no animal-playing-Sokoban recording dataset.

Original source links recorded by the project: [MaleCNS downloads](https://male-cns.janelia.org/download/) and [FlyBrain](https://github.com/HEREISCB/flybrain). The simulator is MIT licensed; MaleCNS data are CC BY 4.0. Attribution and third-party licenses remain in the retained upstream checkout.
